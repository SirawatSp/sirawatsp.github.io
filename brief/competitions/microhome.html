Kingspan MICROHOME 2026

Image source: Buildner

Reviewed 9 October 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Official public pages/FAQs were read; this is an initial decision brief, not a replacement for the full competition rules. Organiser facts and our design analysis are separated. Full Buildner downloadable briefs were not obtained: the pages offer an email request flow, which was not submitted.

Official brief — at a glance

Design a microhome within 25 m² that is efficient, comfortable and context-responsive. Sites may be anywhere; the theme asks how restraint, material honesty and adaptability can support responsible living.

Our design reading

The design test is comfort under a tight area budget. For a tropical scenario, explain shade, ventilation, privacy and storage without quietly moving required indoor functions outside the counted area.

Useful first moves

  • Make an area schedule and draw furniture/clearances at human scale.
  • Use one section to explain ventilation, shading and roof/service strategy.
  • Distinguish a beautiful compact retreat from repeatable, affordable housing.

Fit and first decision

High housing fit, but requires a new tightly resolved scheme and paid registration; this edition is closed to new entrants. If already registered, prioritise compliance and spatial completeness.

Still to verify / risks

  • Full brief is required for Kingspan Award eligibility and product conditions.
  • Buildner Medal publicity on the page is a separate invitation-only award; it is not part of the €100,000 (≈ ฿4,000,000) MICROHOME prize fund.
