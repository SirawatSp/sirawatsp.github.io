Kłodnica Boulevards — Gliwice urban design competition

Discovery lead · 9 October 2026 · Free entry reported by ArchDaily; organiser eligibility unverified.

Illustration source: City of Gliwice / ArchDaily

Source status

ArchDaily announcement read 9 October 2026. It reports free entry and a 12 November 2026, 16:00 submission deadline. Organiser rules could not be fetched through the current network policy; timezone and any separate registration deadline remain unverified.

Design problem

Develop an urban-planning, landscape and architectural concept for the Kłodnica River valley in Gliwice. The city seeks a multifunctional riverside public space, connected green systems, safe walking/cycling routes and climate resilience. This is a city-scale river corridor study, rather than a small building.

Eligibility and fee

ArchDaily lists Price: Free. Whether a Thai-based architect can enter, required professional credentials, team structure, language and appointment conditions are not established by the announcement. Do not describe it as confirmed eligible or zero-cost.

Prizes and deliverables

Prize money, drawing scale, board/file format, registration process and rights/commission terms are unverified. Obtain the official regulations and site materials before preparing a design.

Our design reading

The key task is to organise a continuous landscape and movement system while respecting flood behaviour, habitat and adjacent neighbourhoods. A strong concept demonstrates how public access and ecological repair coexist instead of treating the river only as a promenade backdrop.

Useful first moves

First resolve international eligibility and deadlines. Then map floodplain/ecological constraints, bridges and street access; develop a corridor framework before individual public-space details. Treat travel, translation, local-team input and possible professional appointment obligations as separate potential costs.

Fit and first decision

Promising free-entry architecture/landscape lead, but large scope and a Polish site make it less immediate than a Phuket project. Verification first; do not start design or assume you can enter based solely on the Free label.
