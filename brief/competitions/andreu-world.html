Andreu World — Manufacturing the Future 2026

Image source: Andreu World

Reviewed 9 October 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Official public pages/FAQs were read; this is an initial decision brief, not a replacement for the full competition rules. Organiser facts and our design analysis are separated. Full Buildner downloadable briefs were not obtained: the pages offer an email request flow, which was not submitted.

Official brief — at a glance

Design seating and/or tables for responsible industrial production. Circular materials, efficient manufacturing, durability and social inclusion matter alongside form.

Status2026 entry deadline passed
RegistrationDelivery to Spain: 16 September 2026; US route: 2 September
Submission / resultsSame delivery deadline; jury deliberation listed 29 September
Fee / costsHistorically confirmed free in the studio’s 2 September audit. No entry charge stated in the current rules reviewed; fabrication, shipping, customs, insurance and return costs are borne by the entrant.
EligibilityAll ages; design/architecture professionals eligible. Group work is allowed through one registered entrant.
Prizes€5,000 (≈ ฿200,000) first; €2,500 (≈ ฿100,000) second; three special mentions.
DeliverablesPhysical 1:5 model plus technical report. Every item carries the participant number; no personal identity in submission material. Optional horizontal video: at most 2 minutes, 1080p–2160p, at most 1 GB.

Our design reading

This is a manufacturing proposition, not only a beautiful furniture silhouette. A strong project demonstrates how it assembles, lasts, is repaired and eventually separates into recoverable materials.

Useful first moves

  • Develop an exploded assembly and a material/connection schedule.
  • Prove comfort and load paths at full scale before reducing to the submission model.
  • Use tropical material knowledge as a production argument; do not assume a material is sustainable without evidence.

Fit and first decision

Watch the next edition for a free-entry furniture route. Do not submit to the closed 2026 call. High fit for the architecture–furniture crossover, with substantial fabrication/logistics effort.

Still to verify / risks

  • Current rules include conflicting model-return dates: 2 November–4 December 2026 and an additional 2–30 January 2026 passage. Confirm the applicable window with the organiser if reclaiming a submitted model.
  • Copyright remains with creators, but Andreu World has a three-month preferential industrialisation right after the award ceremony and broad publicity rights. Read the exact legal clauses before reuse/licensing.
